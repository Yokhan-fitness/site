# BUFF IT proof and asset plan -- 2026-05-30

## Rules

- No fake cases, fake testimonials, fake before/after photos, or invented screenshots.
- Proof is split into science basis, service process, safety boundaries, and real client evidence.
- Strength training and walking can be presented as evidence-aligned public-health foundations.
- Do not promise treatment for depression, pain, prostate issues, or medical diagnoses.
