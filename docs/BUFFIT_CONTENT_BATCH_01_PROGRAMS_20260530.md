# BUFF IT Content Batch 01 - Programs v2

Status: rewritten after critique.  
Scope: `programs.html`, `program-weight-loss.html`, `program-glutes.html`, `program-new-image.html`.  
Rule: review document only. HTML is not touched.

## Why v1 Failed

The previous version was too sterile and translated-sounding.

Main defects:

- it sold "route", "support", "new role", and "calm head" instead of direct client outcomes;
- it used abstract wellness language where the page needs a sharp Russian promise;
- it sounded safe, but weak;
- it explained the system before making the reader want the result;
- it had too many phrases that feel like English copy translated into Russian: "тело поддерживает роль", "цель в мире клиента", "маршрут, который видно в жизни";
- it avoided simple sales language: clothes fit better, weight stops bouncing, body looks better in photos, glutes look better in jeans, the person stops starting over every Monday.

The fix: promise first, mechanism second, care third. Not fake magic, but also not academic caution.

## Copy Standard For This Batch

Each page should answer four reader questions fast:

1. Что я получу?
2. Это про мою жизнь?
3. Почему это сработает лучше прошлых попыток?
4. Что делать дальше?

Tone:

- Russian, direct, not translated;
- warm, but not syrupy;
- bold enough to sell;
- no fake guarantees;
- no corporate/product-first wording;
- fewer abstractions, more concrete scenes.

Forbidden phrases for this batch:

- `цель, которую видно в жизни`;
- `тело поддерживает новую роль`;
- `маршрут под вашу жизнь`;
- `спокойная голова` as a repeated filler;
- `собранность` without a concrete scene;
- `wellness`, `lifestyle`, `экосистема` in client-facing hero copy.

Allowed sales logic:

- `одежда сидит лучше`;
- `фото перестают бесить`;
- `живот уходит не через голодовки, а через систему`;
- `джинсы и платье выглядят лучше`;
- `не начинать заново каждый понедельник`;
- `дом экономит время и снижает трение`;
- `тренер держит план, когда мотивация закончилась`.

## Product Frame

Current flagship product:

- 12-week online coaching, optional extension to 16 weeks;
- 2 or 3 trainings per week;
- formats: 2 trainings + homework, or 3 trainings;
- works at home, in gym, or outside;
- home is a strong advantage for time, money, privacy, and consistency, but gym is valid too;
- personal Telegram chat with Igor;
- current included tools: SmartCart/BUFF IT FOOD, protected p2p calls/BUFF IT CALLS, beginner training guide, first-aid/home аптечка guide;
- free diagnostics during launch window;
- prices: 32k/month for 2 trainings, launch 26k/month; 42k/month for 3 trainings, launch 34k/month;
- low-ticket path: one-time 5-8k consult with a science-based training program and one correction after a week.

Client perception:

The client buys a goal. Coaching is the way to get there.

## Cross-Page CTA Logic

Do not make every CTA say the same thing.

- `programs.html`: `Подобрать старт` -> route picker or diagnostics.
- `program-weight-loss.html`: `Пройти диагностику` with `buffit_weight_loss`.
- `program-glutes.html`: `Подобрать старт` with `buffit_glutes`.
- `program-new-image.html`: `Разобрать мой старт` with `buffit_new_image`.
- price-sensitive reader: one-time 5-8k consult.
- not ready reader: guide/channel/materials.
- medical red flag: safety-first route, no sales pressure.

## Risks And Boundaries

Do not promise:

- exact kilograms;
- permanent result without maintenance;
- medical treatment;
- spot fat loss;
- fake cases;
- home as the only correct option.

Safe bold promises:

- work toward looser clothes, better fit, better form, more control;
- explain the mechanism: strength, walking, food structure, feedback;
- say home is often easier and cheaper;
- say diagnostics decides the start.
